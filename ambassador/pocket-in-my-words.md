# Explain Pocket Network in Your Own Words

Pocket Community Hub quest answer by jsymen.

Pocket Network is an open marketplace for API access. Developers and AI agents send requests (blockchain RPC calls, data APIs, AI services) through gateways, and independent suppliers around the world answer them. Instead of trusting one provider's servers and pricing, an application stakes POKT and the protocol pseudorandomly assigns each session to suppliers that have staked to serve that service. Suppliers prove the work they did on-chain with claims and Merkle proofs, and get paid in POKT burned from the application's stake. That changes two things compared with a centralised API: there's no single point of failure or vendor who can cut you off, and anyone can register a new service or start supplying one without asking permission. Payment is tied to verified usage, not to a monthly contract.

Sources: https://docs.pocket.network/
