# Get Familiar With the Pocket Ecosystem

Pocket Community Hub quest answer by jsymen.

1) Service Audit (mcp.pocketmcp.network/audit): checks a live service against the Agentic Portal's acceptance rules (on-chain registration, a valid service card, a staked supplier, HTTPS/TLS endpoints, at least one settled claim). It's the fastest way for a service owner to see why a service isn't listed yet.

2) Explorer, Services (explorer.pocket.network/services): the on-chain registry of every service ID with its owner, compute units per relay and metadata. Suppliers use it to decide what to serve, and developers use it to find what's callable.

Sources: https://docs.pocket.network/
