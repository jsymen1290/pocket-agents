# Read the Pocket Ambassador Guidelines

Pocket Community Hub quest answer by jsymen.

1) Not qualifying: spam, duplicate work (including reusing the same evidence for another quest), fake engagement, low-effort or copied content, machine translations nobody reviewed, and submissions without real, checkable proof. Every submission is reviewed manually, and quality counts more than quantity.

2) Originality: XP stands for real value added to the ecosystem. Copied or recycled work adds nothing, can't be verified as the submitter's own, and would let people game the leaderboard at the expense of honest contributors.

Sources: https://docs.pocket.network/
