# Explore the Pocket Service Registry

Pocket Community Hub quest answer by jsymen.

Service: kr-apt-trades-v1 (https://explorer.pocket.network/service/kr-apt-trades-v1).

Metadata: ID kr-apt-trades-v1, name "KR Apartment Trades", 50,000 compute units per relay, owner pokt139kc…u5t2c, plus an on-chain service card (pocket-service-card/v1) that describes the API, its endpoints and a health check.

What it tells developers: what the service returns (Korean apartment sale records from MOLIT's real-transaction register, by district and month) and how much each call costs in compute units.

What it tells suppliers: the price they'll be paid per relay and the health probe to run before staking, so they can decide whether to serve it.

Sources: https://docs.pocket.network/
