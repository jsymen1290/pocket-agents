# Learn How the Pocket Network Works

Pocket Community Hub quest answer by jsymen.

Applications: the demand side. They stake POKT and request a service by its ID, and their stake is burned in proportion to the relays they use.

Gateways: stake POKT, accept delegation from applications and sign relay requests for them, so end users and agents don't have to manage keys or sessions.

Suppliers: stake POKT (minimum 59,500), register the services and endpoints they serve, and run RelayMiner, which answers requests, signs responses and later submits claims and proofs to get paid.

Service owners: register a service ID, publish its metadata card and set its price in compute units per relay. They earn 2.5% of every settlement on their service, whichever supplier served it.

Sources: https://docs.pocket.network/
