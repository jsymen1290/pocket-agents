# Permissionless Network vs. Curated Agentic Portal

Pocket Community Hub quest answer by jsymen.

1) Registering on the network: anyone can register a service ID and anyone can supply it. There's no allowlist, review or Foundation approval, only a one-time registration fee.

2) Listing on the Agentic Portal: a separate, curated step run by the Pocket Network Foundation. The service must be new (not a near-duplicate of a listed one), not about Pocket itself (that's data.pocket.network), lawful, and pass the Service Audit. You request it by emailing directors@pokt.foundation.

3) The Service Audit: blocking rules A1–A7 (on-chain registration, a valid service card under 4 KiB, unique APIs, an active staked supplier, an HTTPS hostname endpoint, publicly trusted TLS, at least one settled claim on MainNet). A8 (content quality) and A9 (price above the 95th percentile) only warn.

4) Why the difference matters: the chain guarantees open access and payment. The portal is one curated storefront where agents discover and pay for services, so a service can be live on-chain without being listed. A listed service with no requests for more than 30 days may be delisted.

Sources: https://docs.pocket.network/
